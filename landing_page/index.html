Why 84U?

84U is an emergency response app that connects users with dispatchers and responders in seconds. Whether it's medical, fire, or safety-related — help is always nearby.

🚑

Emergency Requests in Seconds

Send emergency alerts instantly with just one tap. No complicated forms or long wait times.

🛡️

Trusted and Secure

⏱️

Fast Dispatcher Response

Connect with emergency dispatchers who are trained to handle critical situations efficiently.

How It Works

Getting help has never been easier. Follow these simple steps to connect with emergency services.

1

Download the App

Install 84U from the App Store or Google Play Store on your mobile device.

2

Create Your Profile

Set up your emergency profile with medical information and emergency contacts.

3

Press Emergency Button

In an emergency, simply tap the red emergency button to send an alert.

4

Help is Dispatched

Emergency services receive your location and details, dispatching help immediately.
